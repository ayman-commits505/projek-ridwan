A rattan workshop that talks to buyers directly

Ridwan Rattan Export is a family-run business in Cirebon, West Java, the heart of Indonesia's rattan industry. We work with local weavers and finishers, and we ship finished furniture to importers, retailers, hotels and home buyers in the United States.

Buyers often ask us the same things: is it in stock, which sizes exist, what does it cost in total, how much is shipping, and when will it arrive. This website answers all of them, so you can plan your order before you write to us.

Shipping rates and transit times

We ship to a US port near you. Sea freight is charged per CBM (cubic metre) and air freight per kg. Transit time is counted from the day the goods leave our workshop.
